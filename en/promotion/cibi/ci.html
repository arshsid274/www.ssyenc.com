Wordmark
CI
Primary
Concept

The corporate Symbol depicting the two soaring dragons intertwining with a smooth and modern style symbolizes the Yin - Yang Balance of Taegeuk, which is the main principle of all creation. Our 'SS' design depicts both key and innovation industries. The left 'S' represents the basic and key industries. The right 'S' represents the technology and service industries. This logo is an expression of our strong will to allow all our domestic and international customers to understand we highly value them. It also shows our company will grow with both the basic/key industries and high-tech/service industries, allowing us to move forward as a world-class leading company.

Color

The gray color of the symbol represents security, safety, and trustworthiness while the imagery of the red color expresses passion and progress as well as the dynamic image from the Balance of Yin-Yang of the Taegeuk

This indicates the combination of the Ssangyong E&C Symbol Mark and Logo Type to form a single symbol mark. It is a representative signature used in various applications and the rules for proportion of the mark and Korean logo shall be strictly complied with.
