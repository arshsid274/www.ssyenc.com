Ssangyong E&C Wins Grand Prize at BIM Awards 2025

Date
2025.10.28


On October 28, Ssangyong Engineering & Construction (Ssangyong E&C) was honored with the Grand Prize at the BIM Awards 2025—an event jointly hosted by buildingSMART Korea (BSK) and Korea Institute of Civil Engineering and Building Technology (KICT).


Ssangyong E&C earned the highest award for its submission on the remodeling project for the Centre Pompidou art museum at the 63 Square skyscraper on Yeouido, Seoul. The entry was entitled “Strategy for Maximizing Remodeling Quality Through BIM-Based Integrated Coordination.”
