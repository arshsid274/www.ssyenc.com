Luxury Landmark

Luxury masterpiece construction completed with craftsmanship and technology

A high-rise luxury complex, the birth of a new landmark

Atlantis The Royal is a super-luxury hotel and residence complex located in the Palm Jumeirah, an artificial archipelago in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. This project consists of three 44-story hotel buildings (total 795 rooms) and three 39-story high-end residence buildings (total 231 units). It is a large-scale project with a total construction cost of approximately 1.254 billion dollars.

Sky Bridge

Unique Design

S-shaped atypical exterior, world's only design

Atlantis The Royal Hotel boasts an atypical exterior curved in the shape of 'S' when viewed from the sky and looks like Lego blocks stacked up.

The exterior, which overwhelms the eyes, has a unique structure that cannot be found anywhere else in the world, and is a masterpiece of luxury architectural art that creates a unique visual effect.

S-shaped atypical exterior

Unique Design

Sky Bridge connecting two buildings

The towers, which are connected by 'Sky Bridge' installed 82m above ground, were installed through a special process of lifting with Strand Jack after pre-assembly.

On the rooftop on the 22nd floor of the hotel, a 90m long Sky Pool connected by the Sky Bridge spreads, providing Dubai's unique skyline and the Gulf view to all rooms.
