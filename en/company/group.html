Group Overview

Global Sae-A Group is a global conglomerate based in South Korea, contributing to help people experience the best in life through a diverse portfolio of industries. These include Sae-A Trading, a global apparel manufacturer and exporter, as well as enterprises spanning the apparel and fashion industry, the paper and packaging industry, construction, infrastructure and technology, and cultural industries.
